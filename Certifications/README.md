## 🧬 1. Structural Bioinformatics & Molecular Dynamics

### 1.1 Molecular Dynamics Simulation with GROMACS
* **Issuing Organization:** BioLab
* **Duration:** 23 July 2026 – 01 August 2026 (5-Day Intensive Training)
* **Credential ID:** `BioLab26072307`
* **Mentors:** Noimul Hasan Siddiquee (Founder & Director), Mahir Azmal (Trainer), Shah Wajed, PhD (Chief Advisor)

![Molecular Dynamics Simulation with GROMACS](MD%20simulation.jpg)

#### Key Competencies & Analytical Workflow
* **System & Topology Preparation:** Preparing wild-type/mutant proteins and protein–ligand complexes in **Linux**, generating topologies (`pdb2gmx`, CGenFF/ACPYPE), and parameterizing force fields (**CHARMM, AMBER, GROMOS**).
* **Solvation, Ionization & Minimization:** Configuring periodic boundary boxes, solvating systems with explicit water models (TIP3P/SPC), neutralizing charges with physiological ions ($0.15\ \text{M NaCl}$), and executing steepest-descent **Energy Minimization**.
* **Equilibration & Production MD:** Setting up `.mdp` parameters and running **NVT** (temperature) and **NPT** (pressure) equilibrations followed by production Molecular Dynamics trajectories in **GROMACS**.
* **Post-MD Trajectory Analysis:** Evaluating conformational stability, flexibility, compactness, and binding interactions via **RMSD, RMSF, Radius of Gyration ($R_g$), SASA, and Hydrogen Bond** metrics, visualized with **PyMOL / VMD** and plotted via **Xmgrace / Python**.

![Conventional PCR, Gel Electrophoresis, Real-Time PCR and qPCR](Conventional%20PCR%2C%20Gel%20Electrophoresis%2C%20Real-Time%20PCR%20and%20qPCR%20based%20Molecular%20Biology%20Techniques.jpg)

#### Key Laboratory Competencies
* **Nucleic Acid Handling & Reaction Setup:** Precision micropipetting, spectrophotometric purity assessment ($A_{260}/A_{280}$), and contamination-free PCR/qPCR master mix preparation with positive and no-template controls (NTC).
* **Conventional PCR & Agarose Gel Electrophoresis:** Optimizing primer annealing temperatures ($T_m$), programming thermal cyclers, casting agarose gels, and resolving amplicons against DNA ladders using a **Gel Documentation System**.
* **Real-Time PCR & Quantitative Expression Analysis (qPCR):** Executing **SYBR Green** and **TaqMan probe**-based assays, interpreting amplification plots, cycle threshold ($C_t$) values, and melt curves, and calculating relative fold-change via the **$2^{-\Delta\Delta C_t}$ method**.
